Principles

Plain over mystical

Clear reading, no act, no fog. A deep night sky and gold linework, calm and legible.

Legible first

Every text pairing meets WCAG AA. Touch targets are 48px or larger. Motion respects reduced-motion settings.

Tarot is always named

Titles, descriptions, the hero and share images say tarot or tarot spread, so the purpose is never a guess.

One source of truth

Change a token in tokens.css, nowhere else. Never use raw hex in a component.

Reader avatar and audio offer

The reader is a nameless figure in gold linework on the night sky, a calm illustration with no character voice. It appears inside the turning zodiac wheel while a reading is made, and on the reading screen beside the audio offer. The offer is one secondary button, 48px tall and full width on mobile, never gold. The avatar ring pulses while audio plays, and the card being read gets a gold rule. All motion stops under reduced motion.

Voice and copy

Plain, warm, even-handed. No persona, no jokes at the reader's expense. Australian spelling. Sentence case headings. No exclamation marks.

Say

  • Your tarot spread, read plainly.
  • Lay out your tarot spread. Get a clear reading.
  • Read my cards
  • Tarot spread readings

Avoid

  • Your spread, read plainly. (does not say tarot)
  • Predictions about health, money or legal matters
  • Mystical claims, "the cards know"
  • Red error styling or alarming language

Every page footer carries: For entertainment only. Not medical, legal or financial advice.
