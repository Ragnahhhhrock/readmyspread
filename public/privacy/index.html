Privacy

You photograph your tarot spread. The photo is shrunk in your browser and sent to Claude, an AI model from Anthropic, which reads the cards and writes the reading. That is the whole journey.

readmyspread doesn't save your photo, your question or your reading. There are no accounts. Our host, Cloudflare, sees basic request data such as your IP address to keep the site running and to limit abuse.

We use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are used. It records page views and basic device and location details, and sets analytics cookies. It is loaded from readmyspread.com, and it never receives your photo, your question or your reading.

Anthropic handles the photo under its own terms and privacy policy. Don't photograph anything you don't want an AI service to see.

Questions about privacy or anything else? Email contact@readmyspread.com.
